Chessie

Guinea Pig · stepmother

Sister of Aria and Marshie I. She later became the gentle stepmother to three nest sisters — Marie "Potato", Freya, and Chi-chi — raising them all as her own.

Marshie I

Guinea Pig · elder sister

A beautiful white cavia, one of three sisters with Aria and Chessie. She carried herself with quiet grace from the very beginning.

Marie "Potato" Brouwer

Guinea Pig · next generation

A brown and white cavia with a nickname to match her sweet, lumpy charm. One of three nest sisters with Freya and Chi-chi, raised together by their stepmother Chessie. She had a way of making every day feel a little lighter.

Angelus

Guinea Pig

Named after the vampire, paired in name with his brother Spike. One of four brothers — with Teddie and Marshie II — and a gentle soul with an angelic name to match.

Spike "Spikey"
